FAQ

My purchased ball or “Remove Ads” is missing.
Tap Restore in the Market. Your purchases are restored from your App Store / Google Play account.

The lights are too intense.
Turn on Settings › Reduced Effects to soften flashes and motion.

Where is my progress saved?
Scores, coins and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes this data.
